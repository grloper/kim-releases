Privacy Policy

Mothlight Archive · Last updated 2026-10-13

What the app is

Mothlight Archive is a fantasy casual memory practice app. Archive Score and related metrics describe practice on in-app scenes only. They are not medical assessments, IQ scores, or clinical diagnoses.

Data we store on your device

By default the app is local-first. Progress, streaks, collection unlocks, and session history live in on-device storage.

Analytics

Debug and early builds may use a mock analytics sink (logcat / local file) that does not leave your device. If a future build enables PostHog or similar, that will be disclosed here and gated by a build configuration key. We do not sell personal data.

Purchases (Keeper)

If you buy optional Keeper cosmetics or convenience (for example lantern-oil freezes), billing is handled by the platform store / RevenueCat when enabled. We do not interrupt an active Restore session with a paywall.

Accounts & sync

There is no required account for the core archive. Optional cloud sync (if added later) will be opt-in and described in an update to this policy.

Children

The app is a general-audience fantasy game. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children for advertising profiles.

Changes

Material changes will update the “Last updated” date. Continued use after an update means you accept the revised policy for that version.
